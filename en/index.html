How it works

Three stages, from PDF to the jury’s table

You don’t post anything until your game passes the first cut. Artwork doesn’t score: mechanics, fun and viability do.

  1. 01

    Reading

    You send rules, photos and a one-page summary. A jury of four readers scores every entry.

    Up to 20 selected
  2. 02

    Playtesting

    Selected designers post their physical prototype. An expert team plays it thoroughly to pick the finalists.

    Up to 6 finalists
  3. 03

    Final

    Four publishing and retail professionals play the finalists and decide the awards.

    1 winner + 2 runners-up

And above all, visibility

The day after the final, the contest’s 59 partner publishers receive each finalist’s sheet: summary, rules, video and the designer’s contact. Published games carry the “Winner”, “Runner-up” or “Finalist Ciutat de Granollers” seal on the box.
