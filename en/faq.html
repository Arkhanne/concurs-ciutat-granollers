Ciutat de Granollers Contest / Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

The quick answers. For anything else, check the official rules or email the organisers.

Who can enter?

Any board game designer from any country, published or not. Only the jury and Àgora del Joc de Granollers staff are excluded.

How many games can I enter?

As many as you like. Each game needs its own form and its own €5 fee.

What kinds of games are accepted?

Any board game except role-playing games, puzzles, expansions and games that need a device or an app.

My game is a print & play, or on a crowdfunding platform. Can I enter?

An online print & play is fine. But until 20 February 2027 the game cannot be published (not even self-published), on any crowdfunding campaign, or under contract with a publisher.

Do I post the prototype straight away?

No. To enter you only send three digital files: rules, component photos and a summary. Only selected games post a physical prototype, by 31 October.

What language should the rules be in?

Catalan, Spanish or English, for both the rules and the summary.

How much does it cost?

€5 per game to enter and a further €10 if you reach the prototype stage. Getting the prototype posted back costs €15 within Spain.

Does artwork matter?

No. Graphic design, illustrations and component quality aren’t assessed. Rules clarity, mechanics, originality, fun and viability are.

My game plays up to 6. Is it worth entering?

Yes, but the jury will play it with at most 4 players. Make sure it works well at that count.

Will I get feedback if my game doesn’t go through?

There’s no written feedback, but you’ll be told your game’s TIER (S, A, B, C or D), based on the reading jury’s votes and points.

Can I improve my game during the contest?

Yes. Selected games may send updated rules until 31 October, and finalists until 10 February (summary until the 15th). Changes so big that it becomes a different game aren’t accepted.

Will I get my prototype back?

If it isn’t a finalist, yes: collect it at the JugarxJugar fair or have it posted back (€15 within Spain). If you don’t pay for the return, it’s treated as a donation to the Àgora del Joc. Finalist prototypes stay with the Àgora.

Do I have to be in Granollers for the final?

Finalists must attend the ceremony (20 February 2027, 6–8 pm) or be available on a WhatsApp video call.

What if a publisher picks up my game?

If you were a finalist, the box must carry the “Winner”, “Runner-up” or “Finalist Ciutat de Granollers” seal with the year, and a copy goes to the Àgora del Joc archive.
