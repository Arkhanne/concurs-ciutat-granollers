Who can enter

Any board game designer, from Spain or abroad, published or not. There is no limit on the number of games per designer, whether solo or as a team.

Jury members and staff linked to the Àgora del Joc de Granollers may not enter.

Which games

Any board game except role-playing games, puzzles, expansions and games that need any kind of digital device.

Until the day of the final (20 February 2027) the game must be:

  • Unpublished and entirely original (plagiarism means disqualification).
  • Not published, either by a publisher or self-published.
  • Not part of any crowdfunding campaign.
  • Not signed with any publisher.

It may be available online as a print & play. Games can be for any player count, but are assessed in games of no more than 4 players.

How to enter

Registration closed

Window: 1 June 2026 to 31 July 2026 at 23:59. Fill in one form per game and attach the three files.

Form · Català →Form · Castellano →Form · English →

If the form fails, you can send the files via WeTransfer to agorajocdegranollers@gmail.com.

Documents: three files

  1. Game rules .pdf

    • Up to 20,000 characters excluding spaces. Images allowed.
    • White background.
    • Must state player count, playing time, recommended age and a full component list.
    • In Catalan, Spanish or English.

  2. Photos of the components

    • Real photos of every physical component of the prototype.
    • Digital mock-ups and screenshots from online game platforms are not accepted.

  3. Game summary .pdf · 1 × A4

    • A complete overview of how the game plays on a single A4 side.
    • Arial 11 for body text and 14 for headings, white background. Images allowed.
    • Not a sell sheet: explain how it plays.

Fees

Entry (per game)5 €By bank transfer once your documents are validated. Unpaid games are not entered.
Stage 2 (selected)10 €Organisation costs. By 31 October 2026, to the same account.
Prototype return15 €Shipping within Spain; abroad, depending on destination. You can also collect it at the JugarxJugar fair.

Stages and TIERs

  1. Stage 1 · Reading — Four readers score each entry from 0 to 4 and vote for their top 20. The 20 with most votes go through; ties are broken on points. Results: 30 September 2026.
  2. Stage 2 · Playtesting — An expert team plays the prototypes and picks up to 6. Finalists: 31 January 2027.
  3. Stage 3 · Jury — Four people from publishing and retail play the finalists and decide the awards on 20 February 2027.

Games that don’t pass stage 1 get no written feedback, but they are told their TIER:

  • SThrough to stage 2.
  • AReceived votes, but not enough to go through.
  • BNo votes and at least 8 points.
  • CNo votes and at least 4 points.
  • DNo votes and fewer than 4 points.

Bands may shift slightly with each year’s scores. TIER A and B can include games with more points than a higher tier: votes come first.

  • In a rigid, well-protected parcel.
  • A box clearly showing the game’s name and every component needed for any player count.
  • No need to include the rules — we already have them.
  • You may send improved rules to concursjocs@granollers.cat by the same date, as long as they don’t change the game radically.

The organisers won’t add any components: if something is missing, the game is out.

What is assessed

Counts

  • Clarity of the rules
  • Production viability of components
  • Market fit
  • Solid mechanics
  • Originality of the concept
  • How fun it is
  • Mechanics that fit the theme (if any)

Doesn’t count

  • Graphic design
  • Illustrations
  • Component quality

Prizes

Winner600 €+ €400 voucher at the Homoludicus Granollers online shop
Runners-up (up to 2)200 €each + €150 Homoludicus voucher
Other finalists50 €Homoludicus voucher + diploma

The jury may withhold the winner and runner-up awards if no game reaches the standard, but not the diplomas. Its decision is final.

Finalists’ commitments

  • Attend the ceremony (20 February 2027, 6–8 pm) or be available on a WhatsApp video call.
  • If the game is published, print the “Winner”, “Runner-up” or “Finalist Ciutat de Granollers” seal and year on the box, and send a copy to the Àgora del Joc.
  • Finalist prototypes become the property of the Àgora del Joc de Granollers.

Entering means accepting the rules in full. The organisers decide on anything not covered.
