Ciutat de Granollers Contest / Publishers
Partner Publisher programme

Publishers

The contest exists to get good games into shops, so every year it connects the finalists with publishers across Spain.

How it works

59 publishers receive the finalists

The day after the ceremony, every publisher in the “Editorial Amiga del Concurs Ciutat de Granollers” programme receives a sheet for each finalist. Anyone interested contacts the designer directly.

Until 31 March 2027, publishers can email to ask for the jury’s detailed opinion on any finalist.

Who decides

Three filters, three teams

The contest is run by Granollers Mercat with Granollers City Council and the professionals of the Àgora del Joc de Granollers. Everyone who assesses games is bound to confidentiality.

  1. Stage 1

    Reading jury

    Four people read every rulebook and summary and pick up to 20 games.

  2. Stage 2

    Testing team

    Board game experts play the prototypes and choose up to 6.

  3. Stage 3

    Final jury

    Four professionals from game publishing and retail decide the awards.
